Processing applied (via @gltf-transform/cli):
  dedup -> weld -> prune -> quantize (position 14-bit, normal 10-bit)

  Original:  175.1 KB (float32 POSITION + NORMAL, unwelded)
  Optimised: 124.9 KB raw / ~27 KB over the wire (brotli)

Draco compression was deliberately NOT applied: at 3,148 triangles the Draco
decoder (~200 KB of JS + WASM) costs far more than the geometry it would save.
KHR_mesh_quantization is decoded natively by three.js GLTFLoader with no extra
runtime payload.

Geometry facts relied on by src/components/effects/car3d/constants.ts:
  - Model faces +Z (headlights at z = +1.72); the scene rotates it 180 deg so it
    faces -Z, matching the camera framing inherited from the old procedural car.
  - Bounding box: 1.871 (x) x 1.203 (y) x 3.927 (z); min y = -0.0157.
  - Wheel nodes are pre-pivoted at their axle centres by the quantize pass, so
    rotating a wheel node about its local X axis spins it about the axle.
  - three.js strips dots from glTF node names: "Cube.006" -> "Cube006".
